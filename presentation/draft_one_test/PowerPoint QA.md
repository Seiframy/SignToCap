The deck was authored with the presentation runtime and animated and rendered in Microsoft PowerPoint 16.0. The 16:9 deck was reopened as saved. All five slides were exported at 1280 × 720 and visually checked. PowerPoint reported that the visible text fits its boxes.

The video was used as a visual reference. The deck follows its serif titles, ruled paper, layered collage and sideways page motion. Deep green and navy replace the brighter accents in the video. The title photograph is an original generated illustration. Depth comes from layered paper and shadows. The project assumes “Norman language” means “spoken language” and shows written output in that language.

PowerPoint's video renderer played the exact final deck. Frame checks confirmed sideways motion between slides, staged reveals in the model and meeting diagrams, and the revised copy on the final slide. Slide transitions are set to 0.70 seconds. Animations start automatically on each slide. Slide advance is manual in the PPTX.

A final file inventory confirmed native editable text, diagram boxes and arrows. The arrows are separate shapes whose positions can be adjusted in PowerPoint. Slide artwork is embedded as replaceable images. Playback was checked with desktop PowerPoint's renderer. Browser and mobile playback were outside this review.
